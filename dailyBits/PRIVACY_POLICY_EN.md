# Privacy Policy for DailyBits

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-01

Welcome to DailyBits. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how DailyBits handles your information and describes our “Local-First” approach to data security.

## 1. Data Collection Philosophy

DailyBits is designed as a secure, private vault for your thoughts. Your journals, images, and audio recordings are stored **only on your local device** by default.

We do not have access to your personal journal content, photos, audio recordings, or private entries unless you choose to share, export, back up, or otherwise transmit them outside the app.

## 2. Information We Collect

DailyBits is primarily a local-first journaling app. We do not collect or store your personal journal content on our servers.

Depending on how you use the app, the following types of information may be processed:

### Personal Journal Content

Your written entries, images, and audio recordings are stored locally on your device. DailyBits does not upload this content to our servers.

### Device and App Information

We may process limited device or app information necessary for app functionality, diagnostics, security, crash reporting, analytics, or advertising, such as:

* Device type and operating system version
* App version
* Crash logs and performance information
* Advertising ID or other device identifiers, where applicable

### Advertising ID

DailyBits may use the Android Advertising ID to support advertising-related features, including showing ads, measuring ad performance, limiting ad frequency, preventing fraud, and improving ad relevance.

You can reset or limit the use of your Advertising ID through your device settings.

## 3. App Permissions

DailyBits may request certain permissions to provide its core journaling features:

* **Camera:** To take photos for your journal entries.
* **Microphone:** For voice input, voice-to-text, or audio recording features.
* **Photos and Media:** To allow you to select, attach, display, and save images or media files related to your entries.
* **Storage:** To save and manage media files locally on your device.

DailyBits does not require access to all files on your device. The app only accesses files or media that are necessary for the features you choose to use.

## 4. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

* To provide and maintain the app’s journaling features
* To allow you to create, edit, store, export, and delete entries
* To support photos, audio, and other media inside journal entries
* To improve app stability, performance, and user experience
* To diagnose bugs, crashes, and technical issues
* To display ads or measure advertising performance, where applicable
* To protect against abuse, fraud, or security issues
* To comply with legal obligations

## 5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

DailyBits may use third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google Play services, Firebase, analytics providers, crash reporting tools, or other similar service providers.

These third-party services may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, Advertising ID, app activity, diagnostic data, or usage information, according to their own privacy policies.

DailyBits does not sell your personal journal content. Your private entries, images, and audio recordings remain local to your device by default.

## 6. Local Storage and Data Security

DailyBits stores your journal content locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including screen locks, operating system updates, device backups, and account security.

If you uninstall the app, reset your device, clear app data, or lose access to your device, your locally stored data may be deleted unless you have exported or backed it up.

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the app, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal journal content with third parties.

However, limited technical, diagnostic, analytics, or advertising-related information may be shared with third-party service providers when necessary to operate, improve, secure, or monetize the app.

Examples may include:

* Advertising and marketing services
* Analytics services
* Crash reporting and diagnostics services
* App distribution and platform services, such as Google Play

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your journal content is stored locally on your device until you delete it, export it, clear app data, uninstall the app, or remove it through device settings.

You can delete your journal entries and associated media at any time within the app, where supported.

Advertising, analytics, crash, or diagnostic information processed by third-party services may be retained according to those providers’ own retention policies.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your data. You can access, edit, export, or delete your journal content at any time, where supported by the app.

You may also control certain privacy choices through your device settings, including:

* Camera access
* Microphone access
* Photos and media access
* Notification settings
* Advertising ID reset or limitation
* App data deletion

## 10. Children’s Privacy

DailyBits is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
